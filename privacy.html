Privacy

A plain summary of what we collect when you shop with Northroom and how we use it.

When you shop or email us, we may collect your email address, order details, and payment-related information processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We may also receive basic site and device information, such as browser type or pages visited.

How we use it

We use this information to fulfill orders, provide tracking, handle returns and support, prevent fraud, and improve the shop.

Analytics and advertising

Analytics are already in use on this shop. We use Google Analytics 4 and the Pinterest tag to measure traffic, understand how visitors use the shop, and measure the effectiveness of advertising. These services may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information about visits, devices, browsers, and interactions. Their own privacy policies describe how they process that information.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on our servers. Stripe’s privacy practices apply to payment data.

We share information only as needed to run the business — for example with shipping carriers, payment processors, and suppliers who fulfill your order. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

The site may use essential cookies for checkout and session function, along with cookies or similar technologies used by the analytics and advertising services described above.
