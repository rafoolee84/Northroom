Shipping

How orders leave Northroom, what delivery usually takes, and where to write if something is stuck.

Where we ship

Northroom currently ships to addresses within the United States.

Processing time

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after payment clears. You’ll receive a confirmation email, then tracking once the package ships.

Delivery estimates

  • US warehouse stock: usually about 3–8 days after ship.
  • China-origin stock: usually about 1–3 weeks after ship (customs can add a few days).

Exact timing depends on the item and carrier. Product pages and checkout notes may show a more specific window when available.

Shipping cost

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your address and the items in your cart. You’ll see the final shipping amount before you pay.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email the tracking number. Use that link to follow the package with the carrier.

Delays & lost packages

If tracking stalls for more than a few days past the estimate, email albazirafal859@gmail.com with your order number. We’ll work with the carrier and, if needed, reship or refund.
