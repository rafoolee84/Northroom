Returns & Refunds

We want you to feel settled with what you ordered. If something isn’t right, here is how returns work at Northroom — plain and simple.

Eligibility

You may return most unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price.

Items should be in their original condition: unused, undamaged, and in original packaging when possible, with tags still attached if they arrived with tags.

How to start a return

  1. Email albazirafal859@gmail.com with your order number (or the email used at checkout), the item name, and a short note about why you’re returning it.
  2. We’ll confirm whether the return is eligible and reply with the return address and next steps.
  3. Pack the item securely and ship it back using a trackable method. Keep your tracking number until the refund is complete.

Please wait for our reply before shipping anything back so we can make sure the address and instructions are current.

Who pays return shipping

For standard returns (change of mind, wrong size feel, or similar), the customer pays return shipping.

If we sent the wrong item, or your order arrived damaged or defective, we’ll cover return shipping or arrange a prepaid label — just include photos in your email so we can sort it quickly.

Refunds

Once we receive and check your return, we’ll issue a refund to the original payment method. Most refunds post within 5–10 business days after we process the return (your bank or card issuer may take a little longer to show it).

Original outbound shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a damaged/defective item.

Damaged, defective, or wrong item

Please contact us within 7 days of delivery if something arrives broken, defective, or not what you ordered. Include your order details and clear photos of the issue and packaging when you can.

We’ll replace the item or refund you — whichever you prefer — and we won’t ask you to pay return shipping for these cases.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene and safety, we generally cannot accept returns on:

  • Opened personal-care or bath items that contact skin (if any are offered)
  • Custom or personalized pieces made to order
  • Items that are damaged from misuse, or returned incomplete or clearly used beyond inspection
  • Final-sale items marked as such on the product page

If you’re unsure, email us before you ship — we’re happy to check.

Shipping note

Orders ship within the US. Delivery is usually about 3–8 days when an item is in US stock, or about 1–3 weeks when it ships from China. You’ll get tracking when your order goes out.

Questions

Write anytime: albazirafal859@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to reply within one to two business days.
