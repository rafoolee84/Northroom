Secure checkout

Payments are processed by Stripe. Card details stay with Stripe — not on Northroom servers.

What “secure checkout” means here

  • Checkout opens on checkout.stripe.com over HTTPS.
  • We receive order email, shipping address, and payment status from Stripe — not your full card number or CVC.
  • Promotion codes (like NORTH15) are applied inside Stripe Checkout when enabled.
  • For order help, email help@northroomhome.com.
