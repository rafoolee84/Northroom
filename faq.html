FAQ

Short answers about shipping, returns, checkout, and reaching us.

How long does shipping take?

We ship within the US. Most items arrive in about 3–8 days when they’re in US stock, or about 1–3 weeks when they ship from China. You’ll receive tracking when the order ships. Full details: Shipping.

What is your return policy?

Most unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. Start a return by emailing us with your order details. Full details: Returns & Refunds.

How does checkout work?

On a product page (or featured card), tap Buy. That opens a secure Stripe checkout hosted through our payment service. You’ll enter shipping and payment there. We don’t store full card numbers on the Northroom site.

Do you ship internationally?

Right now we fulfill orders shipping within the United States only.

How do I contact you?

Email albazirafal859@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 1–2 business days. Include your order number (or checkout email) and the product name when you can. More: Contact.

Who operates Northroom?

Northroom is a small US home-goods shop. See About.
